Met a friend here for lunch on a Friday and we took two seats at the bar because the dining room was full. The bartender was quick with menus and honest about which dishes came out faster. We split the kale salad and the porchetta sandwich; the sandwich was the better call, with crisp skin and enough acid from the pickled onions to cut the fat. The salad was fine but underseasoned, and I had to ask for extra dressing. Coffee was strong and refilled without prompting. It is loud when the room fills up, so do not come here for a quiet meeting. Prices are fair for the neighbourhood and the service was warmer than I expected on a busy day. I would come back for the sandwich and a seat at the bar.
